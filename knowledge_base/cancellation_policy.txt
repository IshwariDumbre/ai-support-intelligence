CANCELLATION POLICY

Customers may receive a refund when an order is cancelled after payment has been captured.

If an order is cancelled before payment is captured, no refund may be required.

When a customer reports a cancelled order with a missing refund, support should verify the order status and payment status before providing a resolution.

Support should not invent cancellation reasons or refund timelines.

If additional information is required, request the order number and relevant transaction details.
