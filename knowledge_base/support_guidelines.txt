CUSTOMER SUPPORT GUIDELINES

Support responses should be polite, empathetic, concise, and professional.

Always acknowledge the customer's concern before explaining the next step.

Only provide information that is supported by the available company knowledge.

Never invent company policies, refund timelines, transaction statuses, or guarantees.

When information is missing, ask the customer for the minimum details needed to investigate.

Never ask customers for passwords, OTPs, CVV numbers, or full payment card numbers.

When a complaint involves a payment or refund, collect the order number and relevant transaction details when necessary.

If the available information is insufficient to resolve the issue, explain that the case needs further investigation rather than making assumptions.
