REFUND POLICY

Customers are eligible for a refund when an order is cancelled and payment has already been captured.

Refund processing time depends on the payment method and financial institution.

Customer support should never guarantee an exact refund date unless the payment system confirms it.

If a customer reports that a refund has not arrived, support should collect the order number and transaction details and verify the refund status.

Support agents must not request passwords, CVV numbers, OTPs, or full card numbers.
